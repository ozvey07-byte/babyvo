Privacy

BabyVO is made by VO Makers. Last updated: November 2026.

BabyVO does not collect personal data, requires no account and contains no ads or tracking. We run no servers.

Video and sound never leave your home. The camera phone's video and sound go encrypted, directly to your other phone (over your local network or a direct phone-to-phone link). They are never sent to a server and never recorded.

Permissions. The camera and microphone let you see and hear your baby on your other phone and talk to your baby. Local network access lets the two phones find each other. Notifications (sound alert, connection lost) are created on your phone.

The pairing code and settings such as sensitivity are stored only on your phone.

Purchases are handled by Apple; we have no access to your payment details.

Deleting the app deletes all its data. Questions: support@vomakers.com
