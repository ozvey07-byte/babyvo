FAQ

The two phones can't find each other. Turn on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on both, and allow BabyVO's "Local Network" permission (Settings → BabyVO → Local Network). Move the phones closer and try again.

Video stops when the camera phone locks. iOS only runs the camera while the app is open. Keep BabyVO open on the camera phone; use "Dim the screen" and keep the phone plugged in.

Does sound play while the watching phone is locked? Yes. Sound and cry alerts keep working while the phone is locked.

What happens if the connection drops? A warning appears within seconds, and after 10 seconds you get a notification and an audible alarm. It reconnects on its own when the camera is back.

Cry alerts come too often, or not at all. Change the sensitivity in the settings on the watching screen. An alert comes when the sound in the room rises clearly above the background noise for a few seconds.

What's in the free version? Live sound, cry alerts and the connection-lost alarm are always free and unlimited. Premium adds live video and talk to baby.

My Premium purchase is missing. Tap "Restore purchases" on the Premium screen.

How do I cancel? iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
